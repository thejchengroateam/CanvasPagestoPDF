Canvas Pages to PDF

a project by thejchengroateam

How to Use

  1. Open Canvas Page: Navigate to any Canvas LMS page (such as Pages, Syllabus, Modules, or Assignments).
  2. Open Extension: Click the Canvas to PDF extension icon from your browser toolbar.
  3. Click View PDF: The extension automatically prepares lazy-loaded images, formats the layout, and opens the preview.
  4. Save as PDF: In the browser print dialog, choose Save as PDF, ensure Background graphics is checked, and click Save.

Things to Know

  • Clean Export: Navigation bars, sidebars, header breadcrumbs, and bottom edit panels are automatically hidden in the PDF.
  • Vector & Searchable: Content remains pure selectable text, preserving copy-paste and search capabilities.
  • Lazy Images Preloaded: Offscreen images and figures are preloaded before printing to prevent missing pictures.
  • No Sliced Tables & Images: Tables, diagrams, and images avoid being awkwardly split across pages when they don't fit.
  • Smart Layout Recognition: Automatically recognizes section headers, dividers, lead-ins, tables, and note cards even when non-standard tags (like styled paragraphs or custom divs) are used, preventing orphaned titles and broken sections.
  • Dark Mode & Themes: Custom themes or extensions (like BetterCanvas) will transfer their custom styling into the generated PDF.
  • LaTeX & Formula Typesetting: Automatically scans and reverse-engineers Canvas formula images, typesets them with the embedded KaTeX engine into crisp vector math, and injects copyable LaTeX code so formulas can be selected and copied as raw LaTeX (note: static screenshots or external pasted pictures of equations uploaded by instructors without Canvas math metadata cannot be extracted).
