Canvas Pages to PDF

Clean, selectable-text PDFs of Instructure Canvas pages. v1.2.0

How to Use

  1. 1

    Open a Canvas page

    Go to the Pages, Syllabus, Modules or Assignments page you want to save.

  2. 2

    Open the extension

    Click the Canvas to PDF icon in your browser toolbar.

  3. 3

    Choose Generate PDF

    The extension prepares images, formats the layout and opens the print preview.

  4. 4

    Save as PDF

    Pick Save as PDF, keep Background graphics turned on, then save.

Things to Know

  • Clean export. Navigation bars, sidebars, breadcrumbs and bottom edit bars are hidden from the PDF.
  • Real text. Output is vector text, so it stays selectable, copyable and searchable.
  • Images preloaded. Offscreen and lazy-loaded images are fetched before printing, so figures aren't missing.
  • Nothing cut in half. Tables, diagrams and images are kept whole when they fit on a page.
  • Layout aware. Headings, dividers, lead-ins and note cards are detected even when pages use styled paragraphs or custom divs.
  • No stranded headings. A section banner or title moves to the next page together with the block that follows it (skipped in Compact Layout).
  • Themes and dark mode. Custom themes such as BetterCanvas carry over, and dark palettes are re-tinted so text stays readable on white. Colored callouts keep their look.
  • Safe on assignment pages. The PDF is built from a throwaway copy in its own document, so Canvas' React pages are never modified or restyled.
  • Embedded tools. Same-origin frames such as the text-entry editor are copied in; external tools (Studio, YouTube, publisher LTIs) become a placeholder box.
  • Rendered widgets. Canvas-drawn graphics and open shadow-DOM content (some maths and custom widgets) are snapshotted from the live page, so they don't turn into empty boxes in the PDF.
  • Formulas. Canvas equation images are re-typeset with KaTeX into vector math, so equations stay sharp and remain selectable text in the PDF. If a typeset formula cannot render (theme or font interference), the original equation image is kept so the formula never disappears. Screenshots of equations without math metadata can't be extracted.
